Most of you signed up back when this was a hobby script that emailed a
handful of flights once a week. Over the past months I rebuilt Air Nomad
Society from the ground up: new site, new emails, and a much smarter way
of deciding which deals are worth your inbox. I want to walk you through
what changed.

Your digest is genuinely yours now. Mark the countries you actually care
about as favorites and every email is guaranteed to carry the best deal I
found for each of them. Alongside those it mixes in surprise discoveries,
destinations you never asked for but might fall for anyway. You choose how
many, and you can rule out whole countries or entire regions you never
want to see.

Every deal now tells you why it made the cut. A short line under each one
explains what it beat: a cheaper connection, a shorter flight, a fare well
under what that route usually costs. And since each deal shows the typical
price for its route, you can spot a real bargain at a glance instead of
guessing. The standout ones get a badge.

No more of the same three cities every week. The digest remembers what it
already sent you, so a destination only comes back when the price is
clearly better, and repeat countries rotate to different cities.

You can fly from more than one home airport. Pick several departure cities
and every destination gets priced from each of them, with the best one
winning. Each deal tells you which city it flies from.

The rhythm is yours too. Weekly, every two weeks or monthly, whichever
matches how often you actually travel. Same for trip length and how far
ahead you plan, up to a year out. There is a link at the bottom of every
email to change all of it in under a minute, and booking is one click:
each deal links straight to the fare.

Now the part where I ask for something. You are all friends and family,
which means you are the only people I trust to tell me the truth about
this. If something is confusing, if the deals are not hitting, if the
emails come too often or not often enough, just hit reply. It comes
straight to me and I read every single one.

And if you know someone who would enjoy a cheap flight turning up in their
inbox, send them to ans.timonrieger.de. It is free, it stays free, and
every person who signs up makes me want to keep building this.

Thank you for being here from the start.

— Timon
